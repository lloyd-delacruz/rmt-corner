About

A small practice, run with care.

Five Registered Massage Therapists, one shared standard. We're not the cheapest clinic on Burrard, and we're not trying to be. We're the one your knees, your shoulders, and your insurance plan will thank you for.

Home·About

01 The Practice

Why we built it the way we did.

RMT Corner started with a simple frustration — too many clinics treat massage therapy like a beauty service, and too many therapists are pushed to see a patient every thirty minutes. That's not the work we wanted to do.

“The patient sets the pace. We listen first, treat second, and explain everything in between — no jargon, no upselling, no rush.”

Every therapist on our team is registered with the College of Massage Therapists of British Columbia. Every appointment includes assessment time. Every patient leaves with a small home plan — one or two specific things to do between sessions. The goal is your body needing us less, not more.

We're a quiet practice. Most of our growth has come by referral, and that's the way we'd like to keep it. If you're booking with us, it's because someone you trust thought we were worth the call.

02 What We Believe

Four principles, applied every session.

These aren't pinned to the wall as a poster. They show up in the way we book, the way we treat, and the way we close out an appointment.

i.

Listen first

The patient knows their body better than we do. Our job is to translate what they're feeling into a treatment plan — not the other way around.

ii.

Treat the cause

The pain is rarely where the problem started. We trace symptoms back to mechanics, posture, and load — and treat the source.

iii.

Explain everything

If we're doing it, you'll know why. No silent treatments, no mystifying "energy work" — just clear, evidence-based reasoning, narrated in real time.

iv.

Make ourselves redundant

Every patient leaves with a home plan. The aim is your body needing us less — not a recurring monthly slot for the next ten years.

Registered Massage Therapist

Joseph Lloyd Tweet

Sports injury & orthopaedic recovery

  • RMT, CMTBC
  • BHK (UBC)
  • Active Release Techniques
  • Graston

Joseph treats post-injury and high-load athletic populations. He works on sport-specific patterns — runners, climbers, court athletes — and post-surgical rehabilitation referred from Vancouver-area orthopaedic surgeons.

Most patients see him for 60- or 75-minute sessions during a recovery cycle, then space out to maintenance every 3–4 weeks.

Registered Massage Therapist

Kyrill Fourth

Deep tissue & trigger point

  • RMT, CMTBC
  • Myofascial Release
  • Neuromuscular Therapy

Kyrill specialises in chronic muscular tension, referred pain, and the persistent knots desk-bound professionals know all too well. His sessions are slow, deliberate, and pressure-tolerant — but never punishing.

If you've left a deep-tissue session feeling worse than when you arrived, his approach will surprise you.

Registered Massage Therapist

Nicholaus Blackburn

Postural & chronic pain

  • RMT, CMTBC
  • Postural Restoration Institute
  • Pain Science

Nicholaus treats long-term postural issues — forward-head, rounded-shoulder, anterior-pelvic-tilt — and the cascade of pain they create over months and years. He pairs hands-on work with simple mechanical cues you can carry into your day.

Patients with chronic neck, low-back, or shoulder pain typically begin with weekly visits, then taper.

Registered Massage Therapist

Mark Todd

Active recovery & mobility

Mark works with athletes and active patients across training cycles — pre-event prep, mid-block flush, post-race recovery. He blends targeted soft-tissue treatment with end-range mobility work that translates back into your training.

Registered Massage Therapist

Therapeutic & relaxation
