Services

Sessions designed around recovery, not the clock.

Every appointment at RMT Corner begins with a focused intake and an orthopaedic assessment. We listen, then design treatment around what we find. Choose the duration that fits your body — not the other way around.

01 Treatment Focuses

One profession, six areas of focus.

All five of our therapists are Registered Massage Therapists. Each works within a clinical specialisation — match your reason for booking with the practitioner whose hands have spent the most time on it.

Sports & Injury

Soft-tissue work for athletes and weekend warriors — strain, sprain, tendinopathy, post-injury return-to-activity.

Postural & Chronic

For desk workers and long-term pain. Neck, upper-back, low-back, and hip patterns that build up over months.

Deep Tissue & Trigger Point

Focused pressure on hypertonic muscle and referred pain. Slow, deliberate, and precise — never punishing.

Active Recovery & Mobility

Between training cycles, pre-event prep, post-race flush. Treatment plus simple mobility homework.

Therapeutic & Relaxation

Slower-paced full-body work. Stress, sleep disruption, anxiety, and the steady hum of daily city life.

Pre & Post-Natal

Side-lying positioning, gentle pelvic and lumbar work, return-to-baseline support after delivery.

03 Your Visit

What to expect at your appointment.

Whether it's your first visit or your fortieth, every session at RMT Corner follows the same considered structure. No rush, no script.

i.

Intake & history

A short conversation about why you're here, what you've been feeling, and what you've already tried. New patients fill out a brief health form on Jane App before arriving.

ii.

Assessment

A focused orthopaedic assessment of the area in question — range of motion, palpation, special tests where indicated. Five minutes that change the next forty-five.

iii.

Treatment

Hands-on soft-tissue work, joint mobilisation, fascial release, trigger-point therapy, or a combination. We narrate what we're doing so you can give feedback in real time.

iv.

Home plan

One or two simple things to do between sessions — a stretch, a self-release, a cue. The goal is your body needing us less, not more.

05 Common Questions

Things people ask before booking.

If your question isn't here, the fastest answer is usually a phone call to 604.341.9060.

In British Columbia, Registered Massage Therapy is a regulated healthcare profession. RMTs complete a 3,000-hour program and are licensed by the College of Massage Therapists of British Columbia (CMTBC). We're trained to assess, treat, and rehabilitate soft-tissue and joint conditions — and to recognise when something is outside our scope and refer accordingly.

Most extended health plans in Canada cover Registered Massage Therapy. Coverage typically includes an annual maximum and a per-session cap. Check your plan or call your insurer to confirm — we're happy to look up your direct-billing options before your appointment if you'd like to email us a benefits card.
