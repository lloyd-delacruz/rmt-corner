# RMT Corner — Welcome Film Brief

A creative + technical brief for instructing an AI video agent (Sora, Veo, Runway, Kling, Pika, Hailuo, etc.) to produce a short welcome film for **RMT Corner**, a registered massage therapy clinic on Burrard Street in Vancouver.

The clinic itself is the source material. Everything in this brief should reflect what RMT Corner actually is — five Registered Massage Therapists running a calm, considered practice seven days a week — not a generic "wellness spa" cliché.

---

## 1. The clinic in one paragraph

RMT Corner is five Registered Massage Therapists on the corner of Burrard Street in downtown Vancouver, Suite 308. Open Monday through Sunday, 7:30 in the morning until 9:30 at night. The practice is clinical-grade but warm: deep tissue, sports rehab, postural correction, trigger point, active recovery, therapeutic relaxation — each therapist has a clinical focus, and treatments are individualised, not assembly-line. The brand voice is *calm, healing, welcoming, premium*. Never sterile. Never loud. Never spa-cliché (no rose petals, no panpipes, no whale song).

---

## 2. The film in one sentence

> A 30-second welcome from RMT Corner — a quiet, cinematic look at a working clinic on a Vancouver morning, scored with a single warm voiceover and ending on an open invitation.

---

## 3. Master prompt (paste-ready)

Use this as a starting prompt in any AI video tool. Edit the camera/lens lines if your tool prefers different syntax.

```
A 30-second cinematic welcome film for a Vancouver registered massage therapy
clinic called RMT Corner. Quiet, observational, documentary tone — closer to
A24 than to a spa commercial. Set inside a sunlit, second-storey treatment
suite on Burrard Street, early morning in late spring.

Open on a wide static shot of an empty treatment room: clean white linens
folded on a low oak massage table, soft natural light raking in through tall
sash windows, a small ceramic vessel with eucalyptus, a folded grey wool
blanket. Hold for two seconds.

Cut to a slow handheld push-in down a hallway lit with warm reading lamps,
past a half-open door — inside, a Registered Massage Therapist in a
charcoal uniform tucks a fresh sheet over a bolster. Their hands are calm,
deliberate, not theatrical.

Cut to a tight macro of a hand pouring warm oil into a cupped palm.
Cut to a four-second push-in on a therapist's hands working slowly across
a patient's upper trapezius — the patient is face-down, draped, only
shoulders visible. The motion is firm, technical, never sensual.

Cut to a quiet wide shot of the reception area: a low oak counter, a
single linen-wrapped notebook, a bowl of unwrapped throat lozenges, a
bell jar of warm green tea. A patient pulls on their boots and laces up.

Cut to two RMTs in profile in the staff corridor — sleeves rolled, sharing
a quiet word between sessions. Real laughter, low volume.

End on a slow exterior pull-back through a window: rain on Burrard
Street, blurred passing cars, a small brass plaque reading "RMT CORNER ·
SUITE 308". Fade to a warm bone-coloured card with the wordmark and
"Mon–Sun · 7:30am – 9:30pm · Book on Jane App".

Aesthetic: warm neutrals — bone, oat, soft sage, ink, a single accent
of leaf-green (#C0D878). Shot on a 35mm prime, shallow depth of field,
slight 16mm film grain, soft window light. No lens flares, no whip pans,
no slow-mo on faces. Every shot held long enough to breathe.
Aspect ratio: 16:9 master, with a 9:16 vertical alt cut for Instagram.
Duration: 30 seconds. End on silence — no swelling music sting.
```

For a **short-form / vertical (9:16)** version (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) — request a re-cut at 15 seconds, recomposed for portrait, with the wordmark/card occupying the lower third instead of full-frame.

## 5. Voiceover script (read in 28 seconds)

A single warm, lower-register voice. Female or male, mid-30s to mid-50s. Vancouver-Canadian or unaccented North American. Slow cadence. Microphone close, low compression, room tone audible.

> **(00:01)** Welcome to RMT Corner.
>
> **(00:04)** Five Registered Massage Therapists, on Burrard Street.
>
> **(00:09)** Open seven days a week — seven-thirty in the morning, until nine-thirty at night.
>
> **(00:16)** Whether this is your first session, or your fiftieth — we'll meet you where you are.
>
> **(00:23)** Take your shoes off. Take a breath.
>
> **(00:27)** We're glad you're here.

**Direction notes:**
- Pace: ~145 words per minute. Don't rush.
- Tone: receptionist welcoming a returning patient, not a wellness influencer.
- Emphasis: *"considered"* feel, not *"transformative"* feel.
- After "We're glad you're here." — full silence over the end card. No sting.

---

## 6. Music & sound design

- **Bed:** a single sustained tone (a struck Rhodes chord, a soft cello drone, or a meditation bell long-tail). Mixed at –22 LUFS, so the voice carries.
- **Ambient:** real room tone — distant traffic on Burrard, a kettle, fabric folding, a door clicking gently shut. Fold these in literally; they're as important as the music.
- **No** wood-flute, panpipes, "spa playlist" loops, swelling strings, or pad music with a reverb tail longer than 3 seconds.
- End in silence, not a stinger.

## 8. Hard "do not"s

- **Do not** show: rose petals, candles arranged in rows, hot stones, sliced cucumbers on eyes, white robes, infinity pools, bamboo wind chimes, koi ponds, lavender oil drips into wide bowls, monks, mountains in the distance, sunsets reflected in still water.
- **Do not** show: a face being massaged in close-up. Always shoulders or back, with draping.
- **Do not** show: nudity, suggestive framing, or anyone visibly in pain.
- **Do not** use: stock-music swells, panpipe music, whale-song ambience, didgeridoo, "Tibetan bowl" tropes.
- **Do not** show: text overlays in any font other than Fraunces or DM Sans.
- **Do not** show: a logo that has a white square behind it, a glow, a stroke, or any 3D extrusion.
- **Do not** invent: therapist names, credentials, prices, or "since 1997" dates. The only verified facts to ever appear on screen are: the address (1160 Burrard St, Suite 308), the hours (Mon–Sun 7:30am–9:30pm), the phone (604.341.9060), and "five Registered Massage Therapists."
