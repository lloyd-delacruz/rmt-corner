01 The Practice

A quieter approach to massage therapy.

In British Columbia, Registered Massage Therapy is a regulated healthcare profession — clinical, evidence-led, and integrated into the wider continuum of care. Every session at RMT Corner begins with a focused intake. We listen, then design the treatment around what we find.

“Care that's considered, not rushed. Treatment plans built around what your body is actually telling you, week to week.”

Our therapists are registered with the College of Massage Therapists of British Columbia and trained to address pain, injury, postural patterns, and the soft-tissue load that builds up from desk work, training cycles, and daily life in the city.

We charge fairly, see you on time, and explain what we're doing and why. Direct billing means most of our patients walk out without paying out of pocket.

03 Why an RMT

Regulated, credentialed, covered.

Massage therapy in British Columbia is a regulated health profession. Choosing an RMT over a non-registered massage practitioner means assured training, insurance coverage, and accountability.

i.

Provincially registered

Every therapist on our team is registered with the College of Massage Therapists of British Columbia.

ii.

Hands-on assessment

Each appointment begins with a focused intake and orthopaedic assessment. Treatment follows from what we find.

iii.

Evidence-led practice

Our methods reflect current research in soft-tissue therapy, pain science, and rehabilitative care.

iv.

Direct billing

We submit claims directly to most extended health plans, so most patients pay nothing out of pocket.
